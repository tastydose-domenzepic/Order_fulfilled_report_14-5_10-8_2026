Send volume vs. open rate

Weekly recipients across all Order Fulfilled flows (bars) against the blended open rate (line). Volume has grown 150x since launch as more markets and 2nd-purchase flows came online — open rate has fallen the entire time, independent of that growth.

Weekly recipients (all markets, OF1 + OF2 combined) vs. open rate
Bars = recipients (left axis) · Line = open rate % (right axis)
Steady erosion
Open rate has declined in 10 of the last 11 weeks — this isn't a one-off dip, it's a consistent downward trend since late May.
Not a volume story
Recipients per week have been roughly flat since early July (~245–265k), so the falling open rate can't be explained by scale — something in list health, content, or deliverability is degrading.

Revenue vs. click rate

Revenue tracked volume growth through early July, then broke from that pattern — recipients kept climbing but revenue and click rate both turned down.

Weekly flow-attributed revenue vs. click rate
Bars = revenue € (left axis) · Line = click rate % (right axis)
The turn
Revenue peaked Jul 6–12 at €49.2k. In the 4 weeks since, it's dropped 33% to €33.0k — even though weekly recipients are essentially unchanged.
Click rate roughly halved
Click rate fell from 1.8% (May 25–31) to 1.3% (Aug 3–9). Fewer opens plus a lower click-through on those opens compounds directly into fewer conversions.
